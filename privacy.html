Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

EasyShoot collects no personal data. Your screenshots stay on your Mac.

What EasyShoot collects

Nothing. EasyShoot has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don’t run servers that receive your data, and we don’t sell or share any data with anyone.

Your screenshots

  • Screenshots and edits are processed on your Mac and never uploaded.
  • They are saved only to the folder you choose, and copied to the clipboard only when you ask.
  • Your settings, shortcuts and presets are stored on your Mac in the app’s preferences.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording lets EasyShoot capture your screen. It is used only when you take a screenshot.

When EasyShoot goes online

  • Fonts. When you download a font in the editor, EasyShoot fetches it from Google Fonts (fonts.google.com, fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com). Like any web request, Google receives your IP address; see Google’s Privacy Policy. Nothing about you or your screenshots is sent.

EasyShoot makes no other network requests.

Children

EasyShoot collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: email haythamasalama@gmail.com or see the support page.
